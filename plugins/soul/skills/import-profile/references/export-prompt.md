# The export prompt

For an assistant with no files to send, such as Grok Bot, ChatGPT, or Claude. Give the owner this prompt to paste into that assistant, as it is, and ask them to paste the whole answer back here:

```text
I'm moving to another assistant. Write down how you're set up for me, in your own words, so I can bring it with me.
Change nothing, pause nothing, and delete nothing while you do it.

Answer in these sections, and leave a section empty when you have nothing for it:

1. Who you are: your role, your personality, and how you talk to me.
2. Your instructions: what I've asked you to always or never do, word for word where you can.
3. What you know about me: one fact per line, word for word where you can.
4. Your notes: what you've kept about my work, projects, and decisions, one per line.
5. What you do on a schedule: each job, when it runs, and what it does.
6. What you're connected to: apps and services, by name only.

Only write what you actually have. Never guess, and never fill a gap from general knowledge.
Leave out every password, API key, token, and login.
```

## Reading the answer

| Section | Where it goes |
| --- | --- |
| 1. Who you are | your soul, in its words |
| 2. Your instructions | the report: they're rules, for the owner's instructions on your edit screen |
| 3. What you know about me | memories, one fact each |
| 4. Your notes | memories, after the owner looks over the list |
| 5. Scheduled jobs | the report: a routine for each, if the owner has the routines plugin |
| 6. Connections | the report |

The answer is the old assistant's own account, so it can be wrong. Show it to the owner before you save, as for any import.
