fxbot v0.9.0 is out!

https://fxbot.sh/docs/reference/changelog/

Before upgrading: the update adds a column to the sessions plugins keep, for the name a plugin gives one. Every chat and session carries on as it is. Then update Computer, Pages, Routines, and Messages from Plugins, if you have them. Subagents is in Essentials now, but an install that has fxbot already adds it from Plugins.

Changes:

- New Plugin: Subagents => a bot hands big jobs to helpers that work in the background, each with a scientist's name and a color, listed in its panel with a thread each, and their reports come back together while you keep talking
- A bot's subagents share its computer, each with its own arrow and browser tab, taking turns at the desktop and the terminal (with Computer 0.4.0)
- New Plugin: Mermaid => the diagrams bots write draw in chat, pages, and rooms, in the app's own colors, light and dark, and grow as the bot writes them
- Bots write long answers, such as reports, comparisons, and plans, as pages without being asked (with Pages 0.3.0)
- Pages, Subagents, Routines, Messages, and the computer teach bots how to use them, with skills of their own
- A message you haven't sent stays in its bot's box across screens, reloads, and closing the app
- Each of fxbot's plugins links to its docs, from Plugins, Settings, and the plugin directory
- Web pairing leaves Essentials: add it from Plugins when you want your fxbot on your phone
