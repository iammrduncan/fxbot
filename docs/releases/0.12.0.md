fxbot v0.12.0 is out!
fxbot: AI assistants that do real work, built your way.

https://fxbot.sh/docs/reference/changelog/

Before upgrading: the update adds one table, for the pills plugins put in a chat. Then update from Plugins: Phone and phone-signalwire to 0.2.0, both, Pages to 0.4.0, Chat rooms to 0.6.0, Messages to 0.3.0, Subagents to 0.3.0, Permissions to 0.1.3, and Computer to 0.6.0. Phone, phone-signalwire, Messages, Chat rooms, Pages, and Web need this fxbot. For Call me, fill in phone-signalwire's new space setting, your SignalWire space's name, and pick a listener and a voice in Phone's Voice. fxbot now reaches SignalWire over TCP port 5061 and sends the call's sound over UDP, with STUN to stun.cloudflare.com: a firewall that limits outgoing connections has to allow them.

Changes:

- Talk with a bot by voice right in the app: the phone at the top of a chat starts an in-app call, and you cut in whenever you like
- Call me is the same call, with its audio over your phone line: same voice, same rules, same bar (with Phone 0.2.0 and phone-signalwire 0.2.0)
- A call is a side conversation with its own chat, and the bot's summary lands in its chat as a pill it remembers
- The bot is a sidekick on a call: it says what it's doing, hands longer work to subagents or herdr, and tells you what they found at your next pause
- A conference call in a room: name a bot and it answers, in its own voice (with Chat rooms 0.6.0)
- Voices from ElevenLabs, Deepgram, a login you already have, a speech server on your machine, or your browser's own; ElevenLabs v4 Turbo sounds the most natural
- New Plugin: Web => bots search, read, and research the web through Keiro, Firecrawl, Tavily, Exa, Brave, Perplexity, and more, or read a page in their own browser (with Computer 0.6.0)
- Format a message as you type, as in Slack, with a format bar, in bot chats, Messages, and rooms
- A page opens beside the chat, so you keep talking while you read (with Pages 0.4.0)
- Drag the edges between the sidebar, the chat, and the bot panel
- Subagents start from a room, and report back into it (with Subagents 0.3.0)
- Bug fix: a skill a plugin gives each bot keeps its name for every bot
- Bug fix: a call no longer takes the bot's own voice for yours, and a bot hangs up only after your goodbye
