fxbot v0.11.1 is out!
fxbot: AI assistants that do real work, built your way.

https://fxbot.sh/docs/reference/changelog/

Before upgrading: nothing changes in the database. Then update herdr, Phone, and phone-signalwire from Plugins.

Changes:

- fxbot opens faster, most on a slow link: no blank page after the bouncing bot, and on a direct connection a reload costs almost nothing
- On a phone, Return starts a new line and the send button sends; a long message can make the box tall to read and edit it, and the box's buttons fold behind +
- /compact shortens a bot's chat to a summary and carries on, with nothing sent
- Bots know when each message was sent, so an old status no longer reads as news, and every summary says what it covers and when it was written
- herdr's screen and an agent's terminal pick up where they were after the phone sleeps (with herdr 0.3.1)
- Bug fix: hanging up a call no longer sends your last words to the bot again, and the bot checks with its tools on a call (with Phone and phone-signalwire 0.1.1)
- Bug fix: "Cannot reach the server" says what answered instead, and a chat from a phone that dropped off lets go of its connection
- Bug fix: an empty message box no longer looks disabled
