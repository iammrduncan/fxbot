fxbot is AI assistants that do real work, built your way. Each one chats, remembers, works on a computer of its own, and runs tasks on a schedule. Pick its model, hosted or local, its look, and what it can do with [plugins](/docs/explanation/core-and-plugins/), and talk to it from any browser, or the fxbot app on your phone, with [web pairing](/docs/how-to/reach-it-from-your-phone/). fxbot is free and open source, and runs on a machine of your own.

New to it? Start with the tutorial, [Your first fxbot](/docs/tutorials/first-fxbot/), which puts fxbot on your phone too, or [ask your AI agent to set it up](/docs/tutorials/set-up-with-an-agent/). Already set up? Put it on [your phone](/docs/how-to/reach-it-from-your-phone/) or [your computer](/docs/how-to/reach-it-from-your-computer/). The docs are in four parts, each for a different need.
